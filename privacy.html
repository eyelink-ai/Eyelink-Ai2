Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Eyelink AI helps people learn about eye health and optical education, and helps patients send appointment requests to eye-care clinics. This page explains in plain language what information we collect, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Eyelink AI is run by its founder. You can contact us at eyelinkai@gmail.com about privacy, technical problems or any request.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and your password. Your password is handled by our sign-in service and we cannot see it.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose Continue with Google, we receive only your name and email address from Google.
  • Profile: your name, phone number and city (optional), and whether you are a patient or an optical student.
  • Appointment requests: the clinic, service, date, time, your name, phone number, city and any note you write. Each request gets a booking ID. We also keep its status (pending, confirmed, rejected, attended, cancelled or rescheduled), any message from the clinic, and whether the clinic recorded your visit and whether you confirmed that you attended.
  • Support messages: your name, contact details and the message you send us.
  • Study activity: quiz scores that you choose to save.
  • AI use: how many AI credits you use each day, so we can keep usage fair.

Your AI questions

When you ask the AI a question, the text you type is sent securely to our AI provider (Anthropic) so it can write an answer. We do not save your AI conversations in our database. Please do not type sensitive details such as passwords, card numbers or full medical histories into the AI chat or into notes.

If you attach a photo of an eye test result or prescription, the photo is sent securely to our AI provider (Anthropic) so it can explain it. We do not save the photo in our database, and it is only used to answer your question. Please cover or avoid showing your name, address and ID numbers when you take the photo. The explanation is general education only and does not replace an eye-care professional.

If you buy Pro, payment is handled by our payment provider, Flutterwave, on its own secure page. We never see or store your card details. Flutterwave tells us your email, the amount paid and the payment reference so we can switch on Pro for your account, and we keep that record.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show you the right screens.
  • To send your appointment request to the clinic you choose, so the clinic can contact you.
  • To answer your support messages and send sign-in emails such as account confirmation and password reset.
  • To keep AI use fair and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • The clinic you choose sees the details of the appointment request you send to it, including your name and phone number. Clinic staff can see only the requests made to their own clinic.
  • Eyelink AI administrators can see appointment requests, their status and attendance records. We use them to monitor referrals, check that visits really took place and prepare monthly reports and referral fees that clinics owe us. Reports show booking IDs, not patient names.
  • Service providers that help us run the app: Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in Frankfurt, Germany), GitHub (website hosting), Anthropic (AI answers), Google (Google sign-in) and Gmail (sending emails).
  • Each account can read only its own records. Other users cannot see your appointments, scores or messages.

Your choices

  • You can change your name, phone number and city on your Profile screen.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and the information linked to it by emailing eyelinkai@gmail.com from the email you signed up with.
  • You can browse parts of the app as a guest without creating an account.

Children

Eyelink AI is not designed for children under 13. Younger people should use it with a parent or guardian.

Health information

Eyelink AI gives general education only. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose. See our Terms of Use.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
